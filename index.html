// The Dataset

The playground is the Marvel Comics superheroes category on Wikipedia — 303 character pages, and a directed edge A → B wherever A's article links to B's. It's dense, hub-riddled, and a little unfair (Spider-Man alone is linked to by a third of the network). For the first half of the course we mine its structure; later, its text.

// The Unit
